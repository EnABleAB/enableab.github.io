01 / About EnABle AB

Science-driven innovation with a future-facing biotech identity.

EnABle AB is building a focused biotechnology platform centered on rational molecular design, functional interface biology, and translational research concepts for high-priority infectious diseases.

Mission

Overcome viral variability

We explore platform concepts intended to remain meaningful even as viral pathogens evolve, helping address limitations of narrowly strain-specific approaches.

Core Strategy

Target functional biology

Our R&D approach focuses on biologically important interaction interfaces that may support robust diagnostic capture and therapeutic design.

Execution

Rapid proof-of-concept research

We combine structure-guided design, recombinant engineering, and wet-lab evaluation to advance high-value early-stage concepts efficiently.
