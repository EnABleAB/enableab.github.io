Technology platform

One molecular platform. Two complementary applications.

Our receptor-decoy strategy is based on viral host-entry mechanisms. The same high-affinity Receptor-Fc protein is being developed for therapeutic neutralization and diagnostic capture.

01 / MECHANISM

Host-receptor targeting

The platform focuses on host-entry receptors rather than viral epitopes that may change through mutation, with the aim of addressing variant-driven escape.

02 / APPLICATION

Therapeutic + diagnostic use

A shared Receptor-Fc molecule is designed to function as a therapeutic neutralizer and a diagnostic capture reagent, creating a unified theranostic approach.

03 / PIPELINE

Programs with defined next steps

COVID-19 proof of concept is complete and US/KR patents have been granted. Development is expanding into Pan-orthoebolavirus and Hepatitis B programs.
