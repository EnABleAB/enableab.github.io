About EnABle AB

Advancing next-generation biodefense solutions from South Korea

Our Mission

To eliminate diagnostic and therapeutic failure caused by rapid antigenic drift, delivering universal protection against emerging and re-emerging infectious pathogens.

Core Approach

By mimicking conserved human host receptors, our platform captures viral glycoproteins at essential functional binding interfaces that viruses structurally cannot mutate without sacrificing infectivity.

R&D Capabilities

Equipped with in-house structural modeling, recombinant protein engineering, and dedicated wet-lab validation infrastructure focused on rapid proof-of-concept execution.

The Decoy Platform

Engineered host-pathogen interface capture system

Mechanism

Broad-Spectrum Binding

Utilizes recombinant soluble receptor domain constructs engineered for optimized affinity and structural stability against target viral entry proteins.

Diagnostics

Equipment-Free POC

Integrated into lateral flow assay (LFA) formats with self-activating sample preparation for decentralized, rapid point-of-care deployment in resource-limited settings.

Therapeutics

Decoy Fusion Modules

Exploring dual-action biotherapeutic concepts combining receptor decoy domains with targeted delivery and enzymatic cleavers to address complex infectious and systemic challenges.
